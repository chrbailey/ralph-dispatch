## PART B — Scout Findings (SI category; candidates NOT in seed list)

**Finding 1 — Trope (YC, 2026). STRONGEST FIND.**
AI-native system integrator for the **Microsoft Dynamics ecosystem** — implements Business Central, migrates companies off retiring Dynamics GP/NAV, runs custom agents inside the ERP as a managed service post-go-live. Agents do configuration, data migration, test cycles, documentation; principal consultants (200+ implementations claimed) scope and sign every gate. Fixed price at scoping, go-live in weeks vs. the industry's 6–18 months hourly. Why the map missed it: YC-stage, no splashy round. Why it matters: it's the Everant model *with a described delivery mechanism and a named human-accountability structure*, in an ecosystem (Microsoft) none of the 7 seeds touch — and the GP/NAV end-of-life wave is a forced-migration budget with a deadline.

**Finding 2 — Anthropic + Blackstone + Hellman & Friedman + Goldman Sachs AI-native enterprise services firm** (announced ~May 2026). The labs themselves entering implementation services. Not a startup to dossier like the others, but it rewrites the map's risk section: the platform-absorption threat now includes the *model* vendors, not just SAP/Salesforce/ServiceNow.

**Finding 3 — Accenture acquired Faculty (UK AI consultancy) for ~$1B.** The incumbent-response case at GSI scale — pairs with the KaarTech pattern.

**Finding 4 — The universe is ~4x bigger than the workbook.** Nikola Lazarov's AI-native service firms database maps 211 companies across 70 industries and 23 countries, $5B+ raised collectively. The 57-company seed is roughly a quarter of the already-mapped population. This database becomes a primary scout hunting ground for the full run.

**Findings 5–11 — Boundary candidates (taxonomy decision needed):** AI-native ERP vendors attacking the *same implementation budget by eliminating it* — zero-consultant deployments in weeks: Campfire ($100M across two rounds in 12 weeks; Accel/Ribbit), Doss ($55M Series B, Mar 2026; Madrona/Premji/Intuit Ventures; operations-first, partners with Rillet/Campfire), DualEntry, Numeric ($51M Series B, IVP), Nominal, Keel, Truewind. They're software, not services — but if the SI budget line is the lens, "no implementation needed" is the most aggressive substitution of all. Recommend a new workbook column: `displacement mode: automate the work | eliminate the work`.

**Scout quota status:** 11 qualified candidates this pilot vs. the 12-candidate quota — full run closes the gap via the Lazarov database, SAP Store, and AppExchange sweeps.
